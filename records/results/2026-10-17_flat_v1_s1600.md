# Flat_v1 s @1600 vs the Mothbox-only model (MBD-1-2)

**Model:** YOLO26s-obb, imgsz 1600, 200 epochs, mosaic 1.0, batch 6, trained on Flat_v1 (5,935 images: Mothbox with fixed
labels in its own split, flat-bug `flat`, 75 Charvalakis sticky-sheet frames). It crashed at epoch 25 in validation NMS
(out of memory on dense sticky cards) and was resumed with validation conf 0.01 (see TRAINING.md gotcha 7). ~13 h total.
Energy for the resumed session (11 h 21 min): 1.76 kWh incl. PUE 1.2, ~26 g CO2e on the BC grid (~15 g/kWh). The report
says 0.84 kg because the resume used the 480 g/kWh default. Run: `/mnt/ami-training/andy/runs/flat_s1600`.
Validation (best.pt, Flat val): P 0.896, R 0.912, mAP50 0.957, mAP50-95 0.738.

All tests use `yolo val split=test imgsz=1600 batch=4` on the cluster.

**Reading:**
- **Mothbox data:** a tie with MBD-1-2 (±1 point, within noise for 170 photos). Adding 4,200 non-Mothbox images
  didn't dilute Mothbox performance.
- **Other setups:** far better: MBD-1-2 misses ~40% of insects on flat-bug data, and Flat nearly matches its Mothbox scores.
- **Sticky sheets:** low precision is expected, because the authors left many tiny specks unboxed, so finding them
  counts as false positives. With only 10 test frames this result is rough.

**Recommendation:** Flat becomes the next Mothbot Process default (proposed name MBD-2-0), after a side-by-side check on
real nights.

Per-source test lists were made with `ls images/test/FB-*` and `ls images/test/CHV-*`, as `test:` txt files in small yamls.
Note Ultralytics' default `max_det=300` is auto-raised when test images hold more objects (up to 686 here).
