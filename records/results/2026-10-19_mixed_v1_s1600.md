# Mixed_v1 s @1600 vs Flat and the Mothbox-only model

**Model:** YOLO26s-obb, imgsz 1600, 200 epochs, mosaic 1.0, batch 6, val conf 0.01, trained on Mixed_v1 (14,775 images:
Flat_v1 sources + flat-bug `other` + YOLO-Fast + 5,408 Fieldguide photos with SAM boxes). 29 h 54 min on the H100 MIG
1g.24gb. Energy 4.63 kWh incl. PUE 1.2, ~69 g CO2e at 15 g/kWh (BC). Logged live to W&B:
https://forge.coreweave.com/wandb/moth-ai/anybug-2/runs/mqcufly1 . Run: `/mnt/ami-training/andy/runs/mixed_s1600`.
Validation (best.pt, Mixed val): P 0.901, R 0.907, mAP50 0.944, mAP50-95 0.709.

| Test set | Model | P | R | mAP50 | mAP50-95 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mothbox test** (170 photos, 1,711 boxes) | MBD-1-1 | 0.867 | 0.840 | 0.906 | 0.603 |
| | MBD-1-2 (Mothbox only) | 0.878 | **0.902** | 0.935 | 0.668 |
| | Flat | 0.897 | 0.892 | 0.937 | **0.673** |
| | **Mixed** | **0.899** | 0.889 | **0.943** | 0.667 |
| **Mixed_v1 test** (1,490 photos, 16,720 boxes) | Flat | 0.875 | 0.793 | 0.876 | 0.649 |
| | **Mixed** | **0.887** | **0.896** | **0.934** | **0.695** |

**Reading:**
- **Mothbox data:** Flat and Mixed tie MBD-1-2 (±1 point, noise for 170 photos). Adding naturalistic/other backgrounds
  costs nothing on Mothbox sheets, and Mixed has the highest precision there.
- **Wider data:** Mixed recovers ~10 points of recall over Flat (Flat misses ~1 in 5 insects on the Mixed test, Mixed ~1 in 10).
- **Recommendation:** Mixed is the most general model and the candidate next Mothbot Process default, pending a real-night
  side-by-side of MBD-1-2 vs Flat vs Mixed.

Note: the Mixed test's flat-bug portion is a different random split from Flat_v1's test (the collect script re-splits
each build), so Flat may have trained on some Mixed-test flat-bug images. If anything this flatters Flat; Mixed still wins.

**Shipped 2026-10-20** as the Mothbot Process default: `trained_models/Anybug-Mixed-v2-0.{pt,onnx,yaml}` (sha256 of best.pt
5e7926f081704dbd…). Process's model finder (`ui/gradio_app.py` `BUNDLED_MODEL_NAME`) and the PyInstaller spec now
accept `Anybug-<Name>-v<maj>-<min>.pt` as well as `MBD-<maj>-<min>.pt`. The highest version is the default (v2-0 > MBD-1-2),
and the dropdown label is the file name.
