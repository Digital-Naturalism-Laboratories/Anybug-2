# Mixed_Open_v1 s @1600 (the publishable mashup) vs Mixed

**Model:** YOLO26s-obb, imgsz 1600, 200 epochs, mosaic 1.0, batch 6, val conf 0.01, trained on Mixed_Open_v1 (9,488 images:
Mixed_v1 minus the 5,287 Fieldguide photos without an open licence; 121 Fieldguide photos kept). 19 h 50 min; 3.07 kWh incl.
PUE 1.2, ~46 g CO2e at 15 g/kWh (BC). W&B: https://forge.coreweave.com/wandb/moth-ai/anybug-2/runs/foo574iz .
Run: `/mnt/ami-training/andy/runs/mixedopen_s1600`. Validation (best.pt): P 0.910, R 0.903, mAP50 0.954, mAP50-95 0.711.

| Test set | Model | P | R | mAP50 | mAP50-95 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mothbox test** (170 photos, 1,711 boxes) | MBD-1-2 | 0.878 | **0.902** | 0.935 | 0.668 |
| | Flat | 0.897 | 0.892 | 0.937 | **0.673** |
| | Mixed (= Anybug-Mixed-v2-0) | 0.899 | 0.889 | 0.943 | 0.667 |
| | **Mixed Open** | **0.907** | 0.888 | **0.945** | **0.673** |
| **Mixed_Open_v1 test** (957 photos, 16,187 boxes) | Mixed | 0.883 | 0.893 | 0.927 | 0.691 |
| | Mixed Open | 0.884 | 0.893 | 0.926 | 0.692 |

**Reading:**
- **These tests:** removing the non-open Fieldguide photos costs nothing. Mixed Open ties Mixed and is at least as good on
  Mothbox photos.
- **Fieldguide-style close-ups** (the Mixed_Open test holds only 14 Fieldguide photos), so both models were also scored on the
  **547 Fieldguide photos in the Mixed_v1 test split**, which neither trained on (`test_fieldguide.yaml`, list `ls images/test/FG-*`):

| Fieldguide test (547 photos, 547 boxes) | P | R | mAP50 | mAP50-95 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mixed** | **0.975** | **0.986** | **0.990** | **0.834** |
| Mixed Open | 0.905 | 0.929 | 0.950 | 0.740 |

  Mixed is clearly better on close-ups. Part of the mAP50-95 gap is box *style* (these labels are SAM boxes, which Mixed saw
  4,300 of and Mixed Open 97), but the recall gap (0.986 vs 0.929) is genuine detection.

**Decision (2026-10-21):** ship **Mixed** (Anybug-Mixed-v2-0) as the Mothbot Process default (done). Publish **Mixed Open** with
the open dataset: it equals Mixed on Mothbox sheets and the open test, and is weaker only on Fieldguide-style close-ups.
