# Mixed_v1 nano (YOLO26n-obb) @1600 vs Anybug-Mixed-v2-0 (s)

**Model:** YOLO26n-obb (2.45M params, 5.6 GFLOPs at 640; ~37 at 1600 vs 144 for s), same data and recipe as Anybug-Mixed-v2-0
(Mixed_v1, imgsz 1600, 200 epochs, mosaic 1.0, batch 10, val conf 0.01). W&B: https://forge.coreweave.com/wandb/moth-ai/anybug-2/runs/1518d4y5 .
Run: `/mnt/ami-training/andy/runs/mixed_n1600`. Training finished, but the end-of-run W&B model upload crashed (root disk
full; see TRAINING.md gotcha 8). The ONNX export and energy report were redone afterwards. Validation (best.pt): P 0.895,
R 0.895, mAP50 0.940, mAP50-95 0.684.

**Reading:**
- **Detection:** nano matches s (P, R and mAP50 within ~1 point everywhere).
- **Box fit:** about 2 points lower mAP50-95 (slightly looser boxes).
- **Against MBD-1-1:** clearly better on Mothbox photos.
- **Speed:** about 1.9x faster on the H100 slice. With about 1/4 of the compute, the CPU / Raspberry Pi speed-up should be
  larger; measure on real hardware.

**Recommendation:** keep s (Anybug-Mixed-v2-0) as the Process default and offer nano as a "fast" option for slow laptops,
previews and on-device use. Process's model menu orders by version, so nano needs an explicit non-default name/handling.

**Decision (Andrew, 2026-10-27):** nano becomes the Mothbot Process **default** as `Anybug-MixedNano-v2-0`
(menu label "default, fast"). The s model `Anybug-Mixed-v2-0` stays as the "tight" option. Only these two are bundled. The old
MBD-0-2 / 1-0 / 1-1 / 1-2 files moved to `Mothbot_Process/trained_models/archive/`; sub-folders aren't scanned or packaged.
Process code: `ui/gradio_app.py` `DEFAULT_BUNDLED_MODEL` + `BUNDLED_MODEL_NOTES`.

## Real-night comparison, nano vs s (2026-10-27)

Both models were run on whole nights at Process's real threshold (confidence 0.25: Process passes no `conf`, so Ultralytics'
default applies; the ONNX path also uses 0.25). Detections were matched by rotated-box IoU ≥ 0.3.

| Night | s dets | nano dets | matched (% of s) | s-only | nano-only | matched-box overlap (median IoU) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tucson DesertHouse/Cactus 2026-08-11 (Process runs) | 1,702 | 1,627 | 95% | 90 | 15 | 0.87 |
| Marburg BioBlitz offpathtree/CutTambor 2026-06-02 (busiest) | 2,777 | 2,496 | 89% | 314 | 33 | 0.87 |
| Marburg BioBlitz bigpole/TalkApe 2026-06-01 (quiet) | 96 | 72 | 71% | 28 | 4 | 0.84 |

- **s-only detections** are mostly real but blurry insects (flies, bees, midges, small beetles; median confidence 0.44 on
  CutTambor), many repeated across consecutive frames (one resting bee ~20 times), plus a few motion streaks.
- **nano-only detections** are a mix of real insects and light streaks.
- **Takeaway:** in practice nano finds ~5–11% fewer detections than s on normal-to-busy nights (more on sparse nights), mostly
  faint or blurry real insects. That's more than the 1–2 point gap on the test sets suggests.

**Decision revised (Andrew, 2026-10-27):** after the real-night comparison, **s (Anybug-Mixed-v2-0) is the Process default**
and nano (Anybug-MixedNano-v2-0) is offered as the "fast" option (`DEFAULT_BUNDLED_MODEL` in `ui/gradio_app.py`).
